# Security policy

Knob captures system audio and watches the keyboard's volume keys, so security reports are taken seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Report it privately through GitHub:

1. Go to the repository's **Security** tab.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Describe the problem, the macOS version, and steps to reproduce.

You'll get a reply within a few days. Once a fix is released, the report can be published with credit to you if you like.

## Scope

In scope: anything that lets Knob, or another process through Knob, capture, record or send audio or keystrokes beyond what's described in the README; anything that makes Knob reach the network; ways to silence a Mac that the escape hatch (turning Knob off or `killall Knob`) doesn't fix.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release gets fixes.
